==========================================================================
Stock Picking: Show Sale Order's Section/Note Lines on Delivery Slip Print
==========================================================================

Shows the related sale order's section and note lines on the validated delivery slip print, grouping the
delivered products under the section they were ordered under, instead of a
single flat product list.

This only applies to the final, validated delivery slip

Configuration
=============
\-

Usage
=====
Print a validated delivery for an order whose lines are organized under
section/note headers to see the effect.
